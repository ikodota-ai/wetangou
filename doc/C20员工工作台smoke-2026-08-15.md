# C20 员工工作台 smoke 链路 · 2026-08-15

## 目标
门店员工 (staff001) 登录 → 工作台聚合 → 今日数据 → 切换门店 → 越权防御,11 个端点 + 3 个防御点 21/21 PASS。

## 端点覆盖矩阵

| # | 端点 | 鉴权 | 覆盖项 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | POST /api/store/staff/login | Anonymous | A 登录返回 storeId/storeIds |
| 2 | GET /api/store/staff/me | @StoreStaff | B me 字段(userType/storeId/realName) |
| 3 | GET /api/store/staff/home | @StoreStaff | C 工作台 5 字段聚合 |
| 4 | GET /api/store/staff/today/orders | @StoreStaff | D 今日订单 |
| 5 | GET /api/store/staff/today/bills | @StoreStaff | E 今日买单 |
| 6 | GET /api/store/staff/today/bookings | @StoreStaff | F 今日预约 |
| 7 | GET /api/store/staff/booking/signup/list | @StoreStaff | G 今日报名人列表 |
| 8 | POST /api/store/staff/switch-store | @LoginRequired | H 范围内 200→101 + 持久化 |
| 9 | POST /api/store/staff/switch-store | @LoginRequired | H- 范围外 200→999 拒绝 |
| 10 | POST /api/store/staff/logout | Anonymous | I 200 (no-op) |
| 11 | GET /api/store/staff/me | @StoreStaff | J member token 越权拒绝 |

## 核心发现
- **J 防御文案**: 返回 `"此操作仅限门店端员工"` (code=403) — `@StoreStaffRequired` 拦截器在 LoginMember.userType != 'store' 时直接拒绝
- **H 持久化**: switch-store 调用 MemberTokenService.refreshToken() 同步更新 Redis 缓存,后续 /me 立即看到新 storeId
- **logout 是 no-op**: JWT 无状态,前端清 wx.storage 即可,后端只返 200
